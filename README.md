An infographic summing up Wednesday 25 November 2026 in one image:
34 verified stories across 6 regions and 13 fields, from Iran's warning to
ships in the Strait of Hormuz to the Sensex's 1,502-point surge — with
illustrated cards, a world map, a numbers strip, a region bar and a DATE
CHECK box naming what was left out and why.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`) and verifies the copy byte for
byte.
